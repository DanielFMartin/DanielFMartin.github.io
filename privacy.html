Farhome Backup Privacy Policy

Farhome Backup is a private application used solely by its owner for personal backups in Google Drive.

Google Drive data

Google Drive data is accessed and used only to create, verify, retrieve, and manage the owner’s personal backups. The application requests the drive.file scope for files it creates or files explicitly made available to it.

No sale or sharing

Google Drive data and backup contents are not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Backups are stored in the owner’s Google Drive account using Google’s storage service.

Security

Backup contents and filenames are encrypted before upload. OAuth credentials, access tokens, and encryption keys are stored privately outside this public website and its repository.

Control

The owner can revoke the application’s access through Google Account settings and remove stored backups through Google Drive.
